Our Goal & Objectives

Computer and Programming Club, Kishoreganj University aims to build a user-friendly competitive environment and ready-to-go helping community to help foster the soft and hard skill needed in the era of technological revolution. Whether you are a beginner who does not know anything about computer and programming or an expert, we have a place for everyone to improve.
